KUU means Keep U'r Uniqueness. Mobile beauty and care in South Florida, with 70% of every payment going to your technician.

She does the work. She keeps 70%.

KuuStore exists to give independent women with real, personally verified skills a steady way to earn. The model is one sentence long, and we print it everywhere: of every payment, 70% goes to the technician.
