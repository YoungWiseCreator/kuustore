How the bundle works

1 · Pick 3, 6 or 9

Any items, any mix — makeup, skincare, hair care, nails, aftercare and body. Tap "Add to bundle" on the cards below and watch your bundle build.

2 · See what you save

Your bundle price is the combined sale total plus 10% for the hunting and ordering — and it is always capped below the combined regular prices, so the "you save" line is real money you keep versus buying at full price.

3 · We order, the store ships

Send an order request (no payment on this page). We confirm the live sale price and availability, then place the order with the store. It ships from that store straight to your door.

One honest heads-up: if your bundle mixes stores — say two items from Amazon and one from Walmart — it arrives as separate shipments, one from each store, because each store ships its own items. The bundle price you see already covers all of it; there is no extra charge for split shipments.
