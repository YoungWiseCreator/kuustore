    Returns, the honest way

    Returns are available on items labeled “Returns accepted” on their card. Items labeled “No returns on this item” cannot be returned, the store that sold it does not take it back, so we never issue a return for it, even if it was bundled with returnable items. The other items in your bundle keep their own return status either way.

    How a return works: contact us from the email on your order to start one. We confirm the store accepts the return, then send you that store’s return label. You ship the item back to the store with that label. Because we placed the purchase, the store refunds our account; as soon as the store confirms it has received the item and is processing the refund, we tell you, with the store’s own stated timeline (for example, 3–5 business days), and we pass the refund through to you, minus a $5.99 restocking fee per returned item. The fee is per item, never per bundle, and it is only ever taken out of a refund the store actually pays.

    Damaged or wrong items are different: if something arrives damaged, defective, or is simply the wrong item, we make it right in full, no restocking fee, ever.
