How much does a facial cost?

At KuuStore, six facials run $95 to $140 — each one a full hour built the same honest way: a real double cleanse, gentle exfoliation, a mask matched to your skin, then serum, moisturizer and SPF to finish. No machines and no mystery products. A $25 booking fee holds your appointment and comes off your total.

What the price includes

We start by looking at your skin and asking what it needs — the mask and products are matched to you, not poured from one bottle for everybody. Strong acids and aggressive scrubs are not our default, especially on melanin-rich skin, where they can leave dark marks. Products are gentle and fragrance-free wherever your skin calls for it, and everything used on you is explained as we go. Full menu: Facials page.

Where the chair is: KuuStore's chair is in Miramar, in South Florida. Prices and care on this page are for everyone to read; the services themselves happen in that one chair — we never imply salons in other states.
