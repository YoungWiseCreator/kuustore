How much do knotless braids cost?

At KuuStore, braids are priced by size and length. Medium braids run $180 (shoulder) to $210 (butt length); small $230–$260; extra-small $400–$430; jumbo and large $160–$190. Smaller braids cost more, not less — more parts, more hours in the chair.

Knee and thigh lengths are priced in the chair before we begin — the price agreed in the chair is the price you pay. Full menu: Hair page. Where the chair is: KuuStore's chair is in Miramar, in South Florida. Prices and care on this page are for everyone to read; the services themselves happen in that one chair — we never imply salons in other states.
