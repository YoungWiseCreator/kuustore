How much does a silk press cost?

At KuuStore, a silk press is a flat $85 — wash, blow-out and press, finished in the chair, set at the middle of the South Florida market. Across the US, a classic silk press typically runs $75–$150; with deep conditioning $95–$160, and with a trim or cut $100–$180.

What moves the price

Hair length and thickness, how much new growth or shrinkage the stylist works through, and whether conditioning or steam treatments are included. At KuuStore none of that changes the number: $85 is the price, agreed before we begin. See the full menu on our Hair page.

Where the chair is: KuuStore's chair is in Miramar, in South Florida. Prices and care on this page are for everyone to read; the services themselves happen in that one chair — we never imply salons in other states.
