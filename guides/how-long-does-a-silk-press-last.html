How long does a silk press last?

One to three weeks — and the honest version is simpler: a silk press lasts until your hair gets properly wet. It is heat alone, no chemicals and no relaxer, temporarily rearranging the hair's hydrogen bonds; water puts them back, and your curl pattern returns at the first wash.

What shortens it

Sweat at the scalp, humidity and steam, water-based products, and one hard rain or one shower without a cap. In South Florida humidity, the shorter end of the range is the honest expectation.

What stretches it

Wrap your hair every night or sleep in a silk bonnet, keep roots dry, tie hair up before workouts, and use a shower cap for every shower. Our Hair page links the full silk press aftercare guide. At KuuStore a press is $85 — see what a silk press costs.
