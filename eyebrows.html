Ombre brows

$500

Soft-front, defined-tail ombre eyebrows, done at the shop and mapped to your face before anything permanent begins. One flat price for the full session, including your consult and brow mapping — set at the middle of the South Florida market.

  • Consult and brow mapping first: the shape is drawn, adjusted with you, and approved by you before the service starts.
  • Colour is matched to your hair, skin tone and the look you asked for: soft and natural, or bold.
  • How long it lasts varies by skin type and aftercare — your technician talks through typical timelines and touch-ups honestly at your consult, before you commit.
  • Aftercare is explained and written down, and a touch-up window is discussed at the appointment.

Drawn-on eyebrows

$20 flat

Not ready for anything semi-permanent? Our brow artist draws your brows on by hand — shaped and filled with professional brow products, matched to your face and your style. It is the drawn look, done by a skilled hand.

  • $20 flat — both brows, drawn fresh at your appointment.
  • Makeup, not tattooing: it washes off, so you can try a shape on before ever thinking about ombre.
  • Made for clients with little or no brow hair, and for anyone who loves the drawn look without the semi-permanent commitment.
  • Shaped with you first: the outline is drawn, adjusted and approved by you before the fill goes on.

Strip eyelashes

$20 flat

The fastest chair in the shop. A full strip of lashes, measured against your eye, trimmed, glued and set by a skilled hand — no extensions and no hours in the chair.

  • $20 flat — both eyes, lashes fitted and set at your appointment.
  • Quick in-and-out: most visits take five to ten minutes, fifteen at the very most.
  • Pick your look first: natural, wispy or full glam — the strip is matched to your eye and your style before it goes on.
  • Temporary by design: wear them for the day or the event, then take them off at home — no fills, no maintenance appointments.
