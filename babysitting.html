Every KuuStore sitting happens in front of an in-home camera, and the feed is yours: open it on your phone at work, at dinner, or at 2 a.m., and see your children, live. That is how we remove the stranger problem: you do not have to take anyone's word for anything.

How watch-anytime sitting works

  • Sittings happen at your sitter's set-up, arranged for children: the camera covers the play and care areas for the whole sitting.
  • You get the live view link before drop-off. Check in as often as you like, for as long as you like.
  • Camera off means sitting off. If the feed drops, you are contacted right away.
  • Sitters' skills and backgrounds are personally checked by KuuStore before a first booking. (Exact screening steps to be published here once finalized.)

Questions

Where does babysitting happen?

At your sitter's set-up, not your home. That is what lets the camera, the childproofing and the routine stay consistent for every sitting.

Who else can see the camera feed?

The feed is for the booking parent or guardian. Access details and recording rules will be confirmed in writing before your first sitting.

What ages do you take?

Age ranges are set per sitter and published on their profile once verified. Age ranges will be published per sitter when babysitting opens — babysitting is coming soon and is not bookable yet, so there is no booking form for it.
