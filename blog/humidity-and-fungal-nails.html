Why Miami's humidity is hard on toenails

Miami gives toenails a hard life. Fungus that affects nails loves warmth and moisture, and South Florida supplies both for most of the year: sweaty shoes at work, sandals in afternoon rain, gym floors, pool decks. Once a nail is affected it often turns yellow or white, thickens, and becomes brittle at the edges.

Habits that genuinely help

Keep feet clean and, more importantly, dry: change out of damp socks, let shoes air out fully between wears, and rotate pairs. Trim nails straight across and keep them at a moderate length. Do not share nail tools, and be choosy about pedicure salons: tools should be sterilized or single-use.

What a KuuStore visit does

Our at-home visits focus on cosmetic care: careful cleaning, shaping thickened nails so shoes are comfortable again, and an aftercare routine built around keeping the nail dry and monitored. We are honest about the limit: cosmetic care improves how nails look and feel, and slow nail growth means visible change takes months. Pain, swelling, diabetes or circulation problems mean a doctor first, always.
