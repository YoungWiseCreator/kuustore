KUU means Keep U'r Uniqueness. Mobile beauty and care in South Florida, with 70% of every payment going to your technician.

Your skill. Your hours. Your 70%.

KuuStore is building its South Florida team: fungal toenail care technicians, ombre brow artists, and babysitters. If your skills check out in person, your bookings can start.

Earnings calculator

Elsewhere, technicians commonly keep roughly $40-$60 of a $100 service. On KuuStore the split is 70%, in writing, on every booking.

Apply

Applications are reviewed personally. The steps are an application, a skills verification in person, and onboarding where the 70% split and scheduling are put in writing.

  • Services are provided at the KuuStore shop: clients come to us, so there is no travel to client homes and no travel fee.
  • Babysitting: sittings happen at your set-up, with the parent-view camera on.
  • You choose your availability. KuuStore fills it; you can decline a booking.

Applications go to the KuuStore team for personal review. Resume upload wires in at launch — please do not attach files here yet.
